COMMON QUESTIONS

THE DETAILS.

What does our venue need to provide?

A suitable visible area, access to a standard 110V outlet and basic placement approval.

Who stocks and maintains the machine?

PackVault handles inventory, restocking, payment setup, system management, maintenance and customer support.

Does our staff need to operate it?

No. The concept is designed to run without adding an in-store staffing requirement.

What happens after the pilot?

We review the placement together. Depending on performance and fit, the machine can stay, move to a better location or be removed.

How is the venue compensated?

Placement can be structured as a fixed monthly fee or revenue share, depending on the location and agreement.
